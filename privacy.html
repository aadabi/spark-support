Spark Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Spark is an affirmation card app made by AI 2 X Co. We built it to collect nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Spark has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't collect your name, email, location or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

Your favorite cards are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us.

Purchases

Card packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Sharing cards

When you share a card, iOS's share sheet sends it only where you choose. Spark doesn't see or store what you share.

Children

Spark doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
