Spark is a deck of illustrated affirmation cards for iPhone, made by AI 2 X Co.

Need help?

Email us at ali@ai2x.io. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

I bought a pack but it's locked. Open Spark, go to Packs, and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Can I use my packs on another device? Yes. Packs are one-time purchases, available on all your devices with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
